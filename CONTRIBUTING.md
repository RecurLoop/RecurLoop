# Contributing to RecurLoop

Thank you for contributing to RecurLoop.

By submitting a contribution for inclusion in RecurLoop, you represent that you
have the right to submit it and agree that the contribution may be distributed
under the Apache License 2.0, the license of this project.

Please keep contributions focused and understandable. Changes should include
relevant tests or examples where practical and should not introduce code or
assets whose licensing is incompatible with Apache-2.0.

For substantial contributions, maintainers may ask for additional confirmation
of authorship or licensing before accepting the contribution.
